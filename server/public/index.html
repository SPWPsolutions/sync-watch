Get Wooper's Party (WP) for Chrome, Edge & Brave

Synchronizes video playback and adds the docked WP group chat sidebar to Netflix, Crunchyroll, Disney+, Hulu, Max, Prime Video, Paramount+, and YouTube.

Download Wooper's Party (.zip)

How to install unpacked in seconds

  1. Unzip the downloaded file to get the woopers-party-… folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the red WP Wooper icon next to your address bar.
  5. Open any show and click the red WP icon → Start the party!

Step by Step

How Wooper's Party Works

1

Install Wooper's Party

Download the WP extension above and load it into Chrome, Edge, or Brave.

2

Pin the WP Button

Pin the red WP Wooper icon next to your browser address bar for one-click access.

3

Open a Video

Go to Netflix, Crunchyroll, Disney+, Hulu, Max, Prime Video, Paramount+, or YouTube and play any show.

4

Create a WP Party

Click the red WP icon and press Start the party to launch the docked sidebar.

5

Invite Friends

Share your party URL with friends so they automatically join in HD sync with chat and emoji reactions!
